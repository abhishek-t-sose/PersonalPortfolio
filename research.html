Paper publishedUncertainty quantification

Bayesian Uncertainty Quantification for Force Fields

Developing coarse-grained and all-atom force fields that come with calibrated uncertainty estimates rather than a single "best" parameter set — combining Particle Swarm Optimization with Bayesian posterior sampling (ESS, AIES, Metropolis–Hastings).

CG-EAM Metals, JCTC 2025 →
  • Coarse-grained EAM potentials for five FCC metals (Pd, Au, Ag, Cu, Pt) with a Gaussian-Process surrogate and Sobol sensitivity analysis.
  • A "True-Tightened / Unchanged / Loosened" classification scheme for judging whether Bayesian refinement actually improved a model.
  • Methodology now packaged as a drag-and-drop browser tool — see the Tools page.
Papers publishedMetal-organic frameworks

Metal-Organic Frameworks for Gas Storage & Drug Delivery

Simulation-driven design of MOFs — from hydrogen adsorption to on-demand drug release — combining classical MD, PSO/genetic-algorithm parameter search, and machine learning across a large functional-group design space.

See full publication details →
  • A PSO-GA framework explored a design space of ~10³⁷ functionalized IRMOF-10 variants for hydrogen uptake.
  • 3D-CNN models outperformed simpler ML baselines for predicting adsorption properties (R² = 0.91).
  • Photoactivated multivariate MOFs for on-demand drug release, published in JACS.
Papers publishedML for materials

Machine Learning for Sustainable Materials Design

Applying ML — from GPR surrogates to explainable AI — to accelerate the search for greener solvents, fluorine-free membranes, and high-entropy alloys, and reviewing where ML has and hasn't delivered in tribology.

Green Solvents, Advanced Science →
  • Assessment, selection, and substitution of solvents using ML property prediction.
  • AI-driven design of fluorine-free polymers for sustainable anion exchange membranes.
  • Explainable-AI-guided inverse design of FeNiCrCoCu multi-principal-element alloys, validated experimentally.

Robotics + ML for All-Natural Plastic Substitutes

The current focus of my postdoctoral work: closing the loop between computational screening and robot-assisted synthesis to discover sustainable, biopolymer-based alternatives to conventional plastics.

  • A combined robotics + machine-learning workflow for composite aerogel discovery, currently under review at Nature Protocols.
  • Self-driving laboratory concepts applied to multi-property tuning of biopolymer composites.

Water at 2D-Material Interfaces

Part of my Ph.D. work: molecular simulations of how water behaves at, and between, layered materials such as graphene, hexagonal boron nitride, and MoS₂, starting from the interaction parameters themselves.

See full publication details →
  • Determination of accurate interaction parameters between MoS₂ and water (J. Phys. Chem. C, 2022).
  • Structure and dynamics of water confined between hybrid layered materials of graphene, BN, and MoS₂ (J. Materials Science, 2022).
